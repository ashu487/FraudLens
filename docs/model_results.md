## Caveats

- PaySim is synthetic and leaks its fraud pattern through account balances, so `full` scores are optimistic. `base` is the honest estimate of what amount/type/time alone can do.
- Time-of-day (`step % 24`) is a real signal in PaySim mainly because its generator makes legit volume cyclical while fraud is not. Treat it with suspicion.
- Velocity rules are excluded from the offline comparison (PaySim timestamps are whole hours).
